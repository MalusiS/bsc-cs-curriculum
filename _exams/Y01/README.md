## Protocol
1. Build a question bank in Obsidian all term (and in Anki).
2. One week before: synthesize it into a formal exam, print it, and have a trusted person hold it sealed.
3. Sit the exam under real conditions.
4. Grade against a published rubric. Below 70% → review and retake within a week.
5. Scan the paper and store here; write a post-mortem: *What did I forget? What surprised me? What do I need to review?*
